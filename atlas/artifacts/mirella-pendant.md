# Mirella's Pendant

## Summary
Mirella's pendant is an inherited oval metal pendant that becomes anomalously active during the Eastgate rift attack. Mirella gives it to three-year-old Asher immediately before hiding him in the maintenance carriage.

## Canon Notes
- **Confirmed origin:** It belonged to Mirella's mother.
- **Confirmed appearance:** Small oval pendant on a dark cord; old, dull metal; shallow markings/lines across its face.
- **Confirmed Chapter 1 reactions:** Turns intensely cold near the unidentified frost entity; thin silver light traces its markings; frost rims the metal; after being placed on Asher it later becomes unnaturally warm beneath his fingers.
- **Confirmed holder at chapter end:** Asher Renn.
- **Intentionally unknown:** Material, age, maker, inscription meaning, original purpose, power source, why it reacts to the entity, and whether the entity is tracking the object or the person carrying it.

## Details
- Mirella kept it even after selling most possessions inherited from her mother.
- Lysandra recognizes the pendant and knows Mirella chose not to sell it.
- Darius cannot read or identify the shallow markings.
- The pendant's first clearly anomalous reaction occurs during the Eastgate attack.
- Mirella places it around Asher's neck and tells him to protect it along with the red wooden horse.

## Cultural Impact
No broader public meaning is established. Within the Renn family, it functions as a surviving material link between Mirella, her mother, and Asher.

## Known Conflicts / Tensions
- Its activation appears to coincide with the frost entity becoming increasingly aware of Mirella/Asher's position.
- It may protect, reveal, attract, identify, or resonate with Asher; current canon does not establish which interpretation is correct.
- The shift from extreme cold to unnatural warmth after transfer to Asher may indicate a change in response to the bearer.
