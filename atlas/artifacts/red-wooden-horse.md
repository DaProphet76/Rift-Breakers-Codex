# Asher's Red Wooden Horse

## Summary
A small painted wooden animal carried by three-year-old Asher Renn throughout Chapter 1. Although its carved horns make it appear to be a cow, Asher insists it is a horse, and the adults eventually accept his naming of it.

## Canon Notes
- **Confirmed appearance:** Small painted wooden animal; red; carved with horns.
- **Confirmed identity in Asher's language:** "Horse."
- **Likely literal carving:** Cow or cow-like animal, based on Sera's observation that it has horns.
- **Confirmed holder:** Asher Renn.
- **Intentionally unknown:** Maker, age, wood species, exact dimensions, and whether it has any significance beyond emotional attachment.

## Details
- One of six painted wooden animals Asher has beneath Mirella's worktable.
- Mirella places it in Asher's pocket before the family leaves home.
- Asher plays with it on the Train Seven windowsill with Sera.
- He keeps it through the attack and separation.
- Mirella places it back in his hand immediately before hiding him in the maintenance carriage.
- It is one of the two objects she explicitly asks Asher to protect, alongside her pendant.

## Cultural Impact
No wider cultural importance is established. Its importance is personal: it is a childhood comfort object, a visual identifier for young Asher, and one of the last ordinary pieces of his life before the Chapter 1 disaster.

## Known Conflicts / Tensions
- The object is visually cow-like but canonically called a horse by Asher.
- Visual adaptations should preserve the joke: the audience should be able to understand why Sera calls it a cow while also recognizing that "horse" is Asher's chosen name for it.
