# Sera's Locket

## Summary
Sera's locket is the approved surviving physical link between adult Sera Dain and her original life as Sera Vale. After she is separated from the Vale family and later adopted, the locket remains her principal tangible connection to her birth family and past.

## Canon Notes
- **Confirmed role:** Personal keepsake connecting Sera Dain to her birth identity as Sera Vale.
- **Confirmed holder:** Sera.
- **Revelation control:** Its full family significance should not reveal the Vale connection before the approved story point.
- **Intentionally unknown:** Material, shape, colour, age, maker, contents, inscription, exact origin, and how Sera retains it through the separation/adoption sequence.

## Details
- The locket is emotionally important rather than ordinary equipment.
- It remains part of Sera's adult personal possessions alongside her military life.
- Visual design must remain open until the adoption/separation sequence and final locket design are approved.

## Cultural Impact
No wider cultural meaning is established. Its significance is private and familial.

## Known Conflicts / Tensions
- The locket must be recognizable enough to serve as a later continuity clue without making the sibling/family reveal obvious too early.
- It is distinct from Mirella Renn's oval pendant; the two objects must not be visually or narratively conflated.
