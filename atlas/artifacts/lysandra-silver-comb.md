# Lysandra's Silver Comb

## Summary
A silver comb inherited by Lysandra Vale from her mother and surrendered as part of the cost of obtaining the family's eastbound travel permits. The comb is not physically present during Chapter 1, but its loss represents the material and emotional price of leaving New Babylonia.

## Canon Notes
- **Confirmed:** Silver comb; inherited from Lysandra's mother; given up before Chapter 1 travel to help secure permits.
- **Current location/owner:** Unknown after being surrendered.
- **Intentionally unknown:** Design, size, maker, age, monetary value, and the identity of the person who accepted it.

## Details
- Darius explicitly remembers the comb as part of the cost of passage.
- Lysandra emphasizes that her mother gave it to her and that she understood its value.
- The permit acquisition also consumes eight years of savings and an unresolved favour.

## Cultural Impact
No broad cultural significance is established. Within the Vale family, the comb functions as a sacrificed heirloom and a concrete symbol of the cost of migration/resettlement.

## Known Conflicts / Tensions
- It is unclear whether the comb was used as an official payment, unofficial payment, barter item, or payment to an intermediary.
- Its eventual fate is unknown.
