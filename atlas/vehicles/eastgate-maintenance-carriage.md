# Eastgate Maintenance Carriage

## Summary
A small maintenance carriage stored in Eastgate Station's repair shed. Mirella uses it as a hiding place for three-year-old Asher during the final sequence of Chapter 1.

## Canon Notes
- **Confirmed location:** Repair shed connected to the Eastgate signal room.
- **Confirmed scale/use:** Small carriage designed for narrow service lines beneath the city.
- **Confirmed condition:** Peeling paint; one side opened for repairs.
- **Confirmed interior:** Rear bench with a tool compartment beneath it; compartment is narrow but deep enough to conceal a small child; slotted metal panel covers the outer side.
- **Confirmed concealment:** Mirella closes Asher inside the compartment and later throws a loose canvas cover over the open side of the carriage.
- **Intentionally unknown:** Manufacturer, age, colour beneath the peeling paint, propulsion method, ownership, and whether the carriage remains in place after Chapter 1.

## Details
- Stored among broken rail equipment, hanging chains, and loose wheels in the repair shed.
- Smells of oil, old wood, and cold iron.
- The compartment allows Asher only a narrow view through metal slots and a tear in the covering canvas.
- The carriage becomes the last physical barrier between Asher and the unidentified frost entity at the end of the chapter.

## Cultural Impact
No broader cultural significance is established. Its importance is narrative and visual: an ordinary maintenance vehicle becomes a child's improvised shelter during a catastrophic event.

## Known Conflicts / Tensions
- The tool compartment is not designed as a passenger space and offers uncertain protection.
- The entity's frost spreads beneath and around the carriage, demonstrating that concealment does not equal safety.
