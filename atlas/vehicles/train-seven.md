# Train Seven

## Summary
Train Seven is the eastbound resettlement train assigned to Darius Vale's travelling party in Chapter 1. It is scheduled to depart Eastgate Station for Eastmere at the second noon bell but is disabled during the rift attack before it can complete its departure.

## Canon Notes
- **Confirmed configuration:** Twelve weather-beaten cars behind a squat iron engine.
- **Confirmed power/era cues:** Steam, coal smoke, pistons, whistle, mechanical brakes, wooden passenger benches.
- **Confirmed assignment:** Vale/Renn party boards car nine.
- **Confirmed conductor:** Pell.
- **Confirmed route estimate:** Two days to Eastmere if the line is clear; four if conditions delay travel.
- **Confirmed fate in Chapter 1:** Forward route becomes blocked by the collapsed bell tower; the train jerks forward only briefly before brakes stop it.
- **Intentionally unknown:** Manufacturer, locomotive class, car-by-car layout, ownership, crew size, maximum speed, and final post-attack condition.

## Details
- Waits on the last platform at Eastgate Station.
- Carries resettlement passengers and their luggage.
- Freight activity around the platform includes sacks, tool crates, and cages of chickens.
- Car nine has facing wooden benches, luggage racks, windows, front/rear doors, and an emergency latch.
- During the attack, windows break, the roof is struck by Rakers, passengers evacuate onto the track bed, and the forward rail route is obstructed.

## Cultural Impact
Train Seven represents one of the limited mechanisms by which Outer District residents can physically leave New Babylonia. For the families in Chapter 1, it is the vehicle attached to eight years of savings, sacrificed possessions, and the hope of resettlement.

## Known Conflicts / Tensions
- Rail travel is vulnerable to damaged lines and infrastructure collapse.
- A valid ticket/permit does not guarantee physical departure.
- The train becomes a trap when doors are locked and the station is overrun.
