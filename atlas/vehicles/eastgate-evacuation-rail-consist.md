# Eastgate Evacuation Rail Consist

## Summary
A small emergency rail consist used to evacuate survivors from the Eastgate Maintenance Yard after Train Seven's main route is blocked. The name in this Atlas entry is descriptive; Chapter 1 does not give the consist an official designation.

## Canon Notes
- **Confirmed configuration:** Small rail engine, two freight cars, and an open passenger wagon.
- **Confirmed location:** Outer track in the Eastgate Maintenance Yard.
- **Confirmed operators:** An unnamed driver releases the brake; porters and guards load survivors. Pell is present on the engine step during the Raker attack.
- **Confirmed passengers shown boarding:** Darius Vale, Lysandra Vale, and Sera Vale among other survivors.
- **Intentionally unknown:** Locomotive type, fuel, manufacturer, exact car arrangement, capacity, destination, ownership, and whether the consist was intended for evacuation before the attack.

## Details
- The consist is visible from the Eastgate signal room.
- Survivors are being forced/urged aboard while Rakers attack the yard.
- Pell uses a coal shovel against a pursuing Raker from the engine step.
- Darius helps defend the consist before climbing onto its rear step.
- The train begins moving while Mirella and Asher remain behind.
- Darius shouts that the group will wait at the East Marker.

## Cultural Impact
No wider cultural importance is established. Its narrative importance comes from becoming the improvised escape vehicle that physically separates the Vale family from Mirella and Asher.

## Known Conflicts / Tensions
- The route and final destination are not shown in Chapter 1.
- It is unknown whether the consist reaches the East Marker safely.
- Do not confuse this vehicle with Train Seven or the separate maintenance carriage used to hide Asher.
