# Asher Renn — Growth Log

## Leveling Rule (Milestones) — Rule B
- **Level-Up Trigger:** major arc beat involving survival, revelation, loss, decisive choice, or transformation
- **Update on level-up:** Class & Level, Proficiency Bonus if changed, HP Max, Hit Dice, Features/Traits, Skills if affected, and Equipment if changed
- **Ability Scores (ASI):** only at ASI checkpoints or a major approved transformation

## Baseline Record (Required)
- **Intro Story Point:** Chapter 1 — age three childhood foundation
- **Starting Level:** N/A at age three; adult mechanical baseline begins in Chapter 2 at Rogue 1
- **Starting Posting/Location:** Age three — Outer District / Eastgate; adult baseline — Outer Ring Districts
- **Core Wound / Core Need:** Fear of visibility and loss; need to learn that survival cannot always come from hiding
- **Core Lie / Core Belief:** Staying unnoticed equals safety
- **Primary Bonds:** Childhood — Mirella Renn; adult baseline — Kade Vale and John Renick

## Timeline Entries

### Chapter/Batch: Chapter 1 — Age Three / Outer District Departure
- **Story Event Summary:**
  - Asher is three years old and lives with Mirella Renn in her home/seamstress workspace.
  - He travels with Mirella and the Vale family toward Eastgate for resettlement.
  - His red painted wooden "horse" is his recurring comfort object.
- **Growth / Change:** Establishes Asher before his adult survival persona: dependent on Mirella, serious/watchful, but still recognizably a small child.
- **Reveal(s):** Grey eyes, dark hair, childhood attachment to the red wooden horse, and strong dependence on Mirella.
- **Cost:** Leaves home and enters a dangerous evacuation he cannot understand.
- **Mechanical Updates:** None.
- **Next Trigger (planned):** Eastgate rift attack.

### Chapter/Batch: Chapter 1 — Eastgate Rift Attack
- **Story Event Summary:**
  - Asher survives the Raker attack while being carried/protected by Mirella.
  - The Vale and Renn families are separated in the Eastgate service trench.
  - Mirella reaches the repair shed with Asher while the frost-wreathed entity tracks toward them.
- **Growth / Change:** Childhood safety collapses into direct exposure to rifts, Rakers, separation, and mortal danger.
- **Reveal(s):** Mirella's pendant reacts anomalously during the attack and the frost entity appears to search toward their position.
- **Cost:** Separation from the Vale family and loss of any safe escape route.
- **Mechanical Updates:** None.
- **Next Trigger (planned):** Mirella hides Asher in the maintenance carriage.

### Chapter/Batch: Chapter 1 — Maintenance Carriage / Pendant Transfer
- **Story Event Summary:**
  - Mirella hides Asher inside a maintenance-carriage tool compartment.
  - She gives him her inherited pendant and places the red wooden horse in his hand.
  - She orders him to remain silent no matter what he hears.
  - Mirella uses a flare to draw the frost entity away from his hiding place.
- **Growth / Change:** Establishes the foundational childhood experience behind later concealment, fear of visibility, and attachment to survival through silence.
- **Reveal(s):** The pendant becomes unnaturally warm beneath Asher's fingers after transfer.
- **Cost:** Asher is left hidden and alone while Mirella leads the threat away; Mirella's final fate is unresolved at the chapter endpoint.
- **Mechanical Updates:** Mirella's pendant enters Asher's possession.
- **Next Trigger (planned):** Recovery/aftermath of the Eastgate event, then the time jump to the adult storyline.

### Chapter/Batch: Chapter 2 — Adult Baseline / Scavenging Sequence
- **Story Event Summary:** Adult Asher begins the primary storyline as an Outer Ring laborer/scavenger/runner and becomes involved with Kade and Renick.
- **Growth / Change:** His established strategy of staying unnoticed is challenged as events force him toward danger and anomalous exposure.
- **Reveal(s):** TBD by current Chapter 2 development.
- **Cost:** Increased visibility and risk.
- **Mechanical Updates:** Adult baseline — Rogue 1.
- **Next Trigger (planned):** Forbidden-sector exposure and later artifact/resonance activation.

### Early Book One — Forbidden-Sector Exposure
- **Story Event Summary:** A scavenging run collapses into danger and forces Asher toward a forbidden sector.
- **Growth / Change:** His normal strategy of avoidance begins to fail; survival increasingly requires decisive action.
- **Reveal(s):** The environment and certain anomalies appear to respond to Asher differently than they do to others.
- **Cost:** Loss of anonymity, increased exposure, and growing danger to those travelling with him.
- **Mechanical Updates:** None confirmed.
- **Next Trigger (planned):** Artifact activation or resonance under mortal threat.
