# Asher Renn — Growth Log

## Leveling Rule
Character growth is tracked when story events materially change Asher's skills, beliefs, relationships, role, or access to anomalous power. Mechanical changes are recorded only when supported by approved story development.

## Baseline Record
- **Intro Story Point:** Chapter 1
- **Starting Level:** Rogue 1
- **Starting Posting / Location:** Outer Ring Districts
- **Core Wound / Core Need:** Fear of visibility; need to learn that survival cannot always come from hiding
- **Core Lie / Core Belief:** Staying unnoticed equals safety
- **Primary Bonds:** Kade, Renick
- **Baseline Disposition:** Quiet, watchful, cautious, reluctant to lead

## Timeline Entries

### Early Book One — Forbidden-Sector Exposure
- **Story Event Summary:** A scavenging run collapses into danger and forces Asher toward a forbidden sector.
- **Growth / Change:** His normal strategy of avoidance begins to fail; survival increasingly requires decisive action.
- **Reveal(s):** The environment and certain anomalies appear to respond to Asher differently than they do to others.
- **Cost:** Loss of anonymity, increased exposure, and growing danger to those travelling with him.
- **Mechanical Updates:** None confirmed.
- **Next Trigger (planned):** Artifact activation or resonance under mortal threat.

### Age Three — Character Foundation
- **Story Event Summary:** Early-childhood material establishes Asher before the main storyline.
- **Growth / Change:** This period functions as emotional and visual foundation rather than mechanical advancement.
- **Reveal(s):** His recognizable identity markers are already present: grey eyes, dark brown complexion, dense dark textured curls, and a serious/watchful demeanor.
- **Cost:** TBD by approved childhood manuscript material.
- **Mechanical Updates:** None.
- **Next Trigger (planned):** Childhood events that help explain his later instinct to remain unnoticed.
