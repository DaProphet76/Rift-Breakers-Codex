# Mirella Renn — Growth Log

## Leveling Rule
Mirella's growth is tracked through story-state changes rather than combat advancement. Updates should record changes in role, relationships, sacrifice, and lasting influence on Asher.

## Baseline Record
- **Intro Story Point:** Chapter 01 — The Last Train East
- **Starting Level:** Civilian / non-adventuring baseline
- **Starting Posting / Location:** Outer Ring home and local lanes
- **Core Wound / Core Need:** Lives in a world where security is fragile; her deepest need is to keep Asher safe and give him a future beyond immediate survival
- **Core Lie / Core Belief:** She can absorb the danger herself if it keeps Asher from having to carry it
- **Primary Bonds:** Asher Renn
- **Baseline Disposition:** Practical, protective, resilient, compassionate, quietly determined

## Timeline Entries

### Chapter 01 — Preparing to Leave
- **Story Event Summary:** Mirella is introduced at her worktable among mended clothing before joining the group preparing to travel east.
- **Growth / Change:** Everyday maternal responsibility becomes active flight and protection as danger closes in.
- **Reveal(s):** Her life is grounded in practical labor, care, and community rather than status or institutional authority.
- **Cost:** She leaves the limited security of home and accepts increasing risk for Asher's future.
- **Mechanical Updates:** None.
- **Next Trigger (planned):** The train attack forces Mirella to choose between escape and drawing danger away from Asher.

### Chapter 01 — The Last Train East
- **Story Event Summary:** During the attack, Mirella raises a flare and deliberately draws the creature away from Asher and the others.
- **Growth / Change:** Her role culminates in deliberate self-sacrifice.
- **Reveal(s):** Mirella's defining trait is not fearlessness but her willingness to act despite fear when Asher's survival is at stake.
- **Cost:** Mirella dies during the attack.
- **Mechanical Updates:** None.
- **Next Trigger (planned):** Her death becomes part of Asher's emotional foundation and later fear of loss, visibility, and helplessness.
