# Mirella Renn — Growth Log

## Leveling Rule (Milestones) — Rule B
- **Level-Up Trigger:** major story beat involving survival, revelation, loss, decisive choice, or transformation
- **Update on level-up:** Role, relationships, injuries/condition, abilities if any are established, and equipment/objects when materially changed
- **Ability Scores (ASI):** only at an approved mechanical milestone

## Baseline Record (Required)
- **Intro Story Point:** Chapter 1 — The Last Train East
- **Starting Level:** Civilian / non-adventuring baseline
- **Starting Posting/Location:** Outer District home and seamstress workspace
- **Core Wound / Core Need:** Lives in a world where security is fragile; needs to keep Asher safe and give him a future beyond immediate survival
- **Core Lie / Core Belief:** She can absorb the danger herself if doing so protects Asher
- **Primary Bonds:** Asher Renn; longstanding bond with the Vale family

## Timeline Entries

### Chapter/Batch: Chapter 1 — Departure from the Outer District
- **Story Event Summary:**
  - Mirella leaves her home and seamstress work with Asher to join Darius, Lysandra, and Sera on the eastbound resettlement journey.
  - She takes only one canvas bag and keeps the inherited pendant from her mother.
- **Growth / Change:** Everyday parental responsibility becomes deliberate flight from a city she no longer considers acceptable for Asher's childhood.
- **Reveal(s):** Mirella has preserved one family heirloom even after selling or abandoning most other inherited possessions.
- **Cost:** Leaves home, livelihood, and most possessions behind.
- **Mechanical Updates:** None.
- **Next Trigger (planned):** Eastgate rift attack.

### Chapter/Batch: Chapter 1 — Eastgate Rift Attack
- **Story Event Summary:**
  - Mirella fights to protect Asher and the Vale family as Rakers overrun Eastgate Station.
  - She identifies the service-trench escape route through knowledge gained from repairing trench-crew uniforms.
  - A steam-pipe collapse separates Mirella and Asher from Darius, Lysandra, and Sera.
  - Mirella's pendant becomes anomalously cold and luminous as the frost-wreathed entity searches the station.
- **Growth / Change:** Mirella shifts from escape-oriented survival to active confrontation and improvisation.
- **Reveal(s):** The pendant has an unexplained anomalous response; the frost entity appears connected to Mirella, Asher, or the pendant.
- **Cost:** Separation from the Vale family; worsening danger to Asher; loss of ordinary escape route.
- **Mechanical Updates:** None confirmed.
- **Next Trigger (planned):** Protect Asher in the repair shed.

### Chapter/Batch: Chapter 1 — Repair Shed Diversion
- **Story Event Summary:**
  - Mirella hides Asher inside the tool compartment of a maintenance carriage.
  - She transfers her mother's pendant to Asher and leaves him with the red wooden horse.
  - Using a signal flare, she steps between Asher and the frost entity and deliberately leads it away from the carriage into the ruined yard.
- **Growth / Change:** Her protective role reaches its most extreme current point: she chooses separation and personal exposure to give Asher a chance to survive.
- **Reveal(s):** The pendant changes from freezing cold near Mirella to unnatural warmth beneath Asher's hand after transfer.
- **Cost:** Mirella is separated from Asher and places herself directly in the entity's path.
- **Mechanical Updates:** Pendant no longer in Mirella's possession; transferred to Asher.
- **Next Trigger (planned):** Mirella's final outcome remains unresolved after the Chapter 1 endpoint.
