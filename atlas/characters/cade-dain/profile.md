# Cade Dain

## Character Profile (Story-Facing)

### Quick Identity
- **Full Name:** Cade Dain
- **Aliases:** Cade; legacy material uses the spelling Kade
- **Origin / District:** Outer Ring
- **Age:** Adult; exact age TBD
- **Role in Story:** Scavenger, field tactician, and Sera Dain's brother

### Appearance
Cade has a lean, athletic build, warm tan-brown skin, brown eyes, short dark textured hair, and a visible scar along the right cheek. His family resemblance to Sera is strongest through complexion, eyes, and facial structure. In the field he favors a hood, face covering, weathered cloak, scavenged armor pieces, layered dark clothing, gloves, utility pouches, worn boots, and a compact pack. His silhouette is intentionally low-profile and practical rather than military.

### Personality
- **Strengths:** Observant, decisive under pressure, resourceful, loyal once trust is earned
- **Flaws:** Guarded, risk-tolerant, controlling when afraid, quick to anticipate betrayal
- **Fears:** Being trapped without an exit; losing autonomy; failing someone he chose to protect
- **Desires:** Freedom of movement, control over his own life, and enough leverage to keep his people out of institutional control

### Background
Cade was shaped by Outer Ring survival culture: checkpoints, shortages, route knowledge, rival scavenger lanes, and an economy built around salvage, favors, and information. He learned to survive through stealth, preparation, and disciplined movement rather than formal military training. He is Sera Dain's brother, but their paths diverged: Sera entered structured security service while Cade remained outside institutional authority and built his life in the scavenger networks.

### Motivations
- **Short-term:** Complete profitable scavenging runs, avoid unnecessary exposure, and keep his group alive
- **Long-term:** Build enough independence and leverage that neither he nor the people close to him can be easily controlled by the city

### Relationships
- **Sera Dain:** Sister; shared family history but sharply different relationships with authority and structure
- **Asher Renn:** Acquaintance-turned-ally; Cade draws Asher into the scavenger lanes and early danger
- **Rennick:** Experienced fellow survivor; cooperation is practical, with underlying friction and mutual respect

### Arc
- **Start:** Competent scavenger who believes speed, preparation, and control can solve most problems
- **Pressure:** Rift events, forbidden sectors, and threats that cannot be managed through normal scavenger tactics
- **Change:** Learns that survival sometimes depends on trust, sacrifice, and accepting uncertainty
- **End state (planned):** TBD

### Secrets / Reveals
- Cade knows more about certain scavenger routes and forbidden-sector boundaries than he openly shares
- The full reason for his resistance to sponsorship, testing, and institutional processing remains TBD
