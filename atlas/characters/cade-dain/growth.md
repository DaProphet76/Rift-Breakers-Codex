# Cade Dain — Growth Log

## Leveling Rule (Milestones) — Rule B
- **Level-Up Trigger:** major arc beat involving survival, revelation, loss, decisive choice, or transformation
- **Update on level-up:** Class & Level, Proficiency Bonus if changed, HP Max, Hit Dice, Features/Traits, Skills if affected, and Equipment if changed
- **Ability Scores (ASI):** only at ASI checkpoints or a major approved transformation

## Baseline Record (Required)
- **Intro Story Point:** Chapter 2 — scavenging sequence
- **Starting Level:** Rogue 2
- **Starting Posting/Location:** Outer Ring scavenger lanes
- **Core Wound / Core Need:** Needs control and a reliable exit path in a world where systems can close around people without warning
- **Core Lie / Core Belief:** "If I move fast, stay sharp, and keep control, nothing can take me."
- **Primary Bonds:** Sera Dain, Asher Renn, Rennick

## Timeline Entries

### Chapter/Batch: Chapter 2 — Scavenging Run
- **Story Event Summary:**
  - Cade draws Asher into a scavenging run during tightened district conditions.
  - The run crosses dangerous territory, breaks apart under violence, and leaves Cade, Asher, and Rennick together.
  - The surviving trio is forced toward forbidden-sector exposure.
- **Growth / Change:** Cade's normal reliance on planning, speed, and route control begins to fail against threats that do not follow ordinary scavenger rules.
- **Reveal(s):** His knowledge of dangerous routes and boundaries is broader than he initially admits.
- **Cost:** Loss of other members of the scavenging group; reduced control over the route and outcome.
- **Mechanical Updates:** None confirmed.
- **Next Trigger (planned):** A rift or Wraith encounter forces Cade to choose between personal escape, salvage, and protecting the people beside him.
