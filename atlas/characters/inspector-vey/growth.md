## Timeline Entries

### Chapter/Batch: Chapter 1 — Checkpoint Inspection
- **Story Event Summary:** Vey inspects the Vale/Renn party's documents, confirms their unusual labour exemptions, stamps the permits, and directs them to Train Seven.
- **Growth / Change:** None yet; Vey begins as institutional authority functioning through procedure.
- **Reveal(s):** He is exacting but does ultimately honor valid documentation.
- **Cost:** None established.
- **Mechanical Updates:** None.
- **Next Trigger (planned):** Rift attack.

### Chapter/Batch: Chapter 1 — Eastgate Rift Attack
- **Story Event Summary:**
  - Vey orders the Eastern Gate sealed and joins the armed defense against Rakers.
  - He initially refuses Darius access to the service trench because the trench gate is supposed to remain sealed.
  - A Raker injures him, tearing his coat and flesh.
  - Vey gives Darius his key ring and identifies the third key, marked blue, as the trench key.
  - When offered escape, Vey refuses and returns toward the gate, saying he still has a gate to close.
- **Growth / Change:** Procedure yields to a personal decision that enables civilians to escape, even while Vey remains committed to his own duty at Eastgate.
- **Reveal(s):** His commitment to the gate is more than bureaucratic self-preservation; he stays after giving others a way out.
- **Cost:** Significant injury; loss of control over the checkpoint; final fate unresolved.
- **Mechanical Updates:** Injured/limping; key ring transferred to Darius.
- **Next Trigger (planned):** TBD if Vey survives or reappears after Chapter 1.
