# Sera Dain — Growth Log

## Leveling Rule (Milestones) — Rule B
- **Level-Up Trigger:** major arc beat involving survival, revelation, loss, decisive choice, or transformation
- **Update on level-up:** Class & Level, Proficiency Bonus if changed, HP Max, Hit Dice, Features/Traits, Skills if affected, and Equipment if changed
- **Ability Scores (ASI):** only at ASI checkpoints or a major approved transformation

## Baseline Record (Required)
- **Intro Story Point:** Chapter 1 as child Sera Vale; adult mechanical baseline begins at the Dock Outpost introduction
- **Starting Level:** Child baseline N/A; adult baseline Fighter 4 (Battle Master)
- **Starting Posting/Location:** Childhood — Outer District / Eastgate; adult — Outer Ring Dock Outpost
- **Core Wound / Core Need:** Fear of losing people under her command; need to learn that responsibility cannot be reduced to control and procedure
- **Core Lie / Core Belief:** "If I maintain discipline and control, I can keep everyone alive."
- **Primary Bonds:** Childhood — Darius Vale, Lysandra Vale, unborn Kade Vale; adult — Dock Outpost Detachment, hidden birth-family bond

## Timeline Entries

### Chapter/Batch: Chapter 1 — Child Foundation / Sera Vale
- **Story Event Summary:**
  - Sera is introduced as Darius and Lysandra Vale's young daughter during the family's attempted resettlement journey.
  - She has dressed herself for travel: one boot properly tied, the other untied, and a blue scarf wrapped repeatedly around her neck.
  - She interacts with three-year-old Asher over his red wooden animal, insisting it is a cow before eventually accepting his name for it: "horse."
  - She survives the Eastgate rift attack, follows her parents through the service trench, and escapes the maintenance yard aboard the small rail consist.
- **Growth / Change:** Establishes the child who later becomes Lieutenant Dain before military identity, adoption, or command discipline shape her.
- **Reveal(s):** Her birth identity is Sera Vale; she is the daughter of Darius and Lysandra and the older sister of the unborn Kade Vale.
- **Cost:** The planned journey collapses into a mass-casualty rift event; she is separated from Mirella and Asher during the escape.
- **Mechanical Updates:** None.
- **Next Trigger (planned):** Later separation from her own birth family during the continuing evacuation crisis.

### Planned Chapter/Batch: Vale Family Separation and Adoption
- **Story Event Summary:** During the continuing evacuation crisis after the current Chapter 1 endpoint, Sera becomes separated from Darius and Lysandra and is later taken in/adopted within the city.
- **Growth / Change:** Loss of birth-family context becomes a foundational source of abandonment fear, self-reliance, and later attachment to structure.
- **Reveal(s):** Sera Vale becomes Sera Dain; her locket remains the principal physical link to her birth family.
- **Cost:** Long-term separation from the Vale family and loss of her original surname/identity context.
- **Mechanical Updates:** None.
- **Next Trigger (planned):** Childhood/adolescent material establishing the adoptive household and eventual decision to enter military service.

### Chapter/Batch: Adult Introduction — Dock Rift Event
- **Story Event Summary:** Sera is on active duty at the Outer Ring Dock Outpost when a rift manifestation disrupts normal security conditions.
- **Growth / Change:** Her confidence in procedure begins to fracture as she is forced to improvise and prioritize people over doctrine.
- **Reveal(s):** The institution cannot guarantee the safety or truth it presents itself as controlling.
- **Cost:** Increased personal responsibility for casualties, unit survival, and decisions made outside approved procedure.
- **Mechanical Updates:** None confirmed.
- **Next Trigger (planned):** A future command decision forces Sera to choose between institutional orders and protecting the people she considers her responsibility.

### Planned Reveal — Vale Sibling Connection
- **Story Event Summary:** Sera's biological relationship to Kade is revealed later, after she has first been established to the group as Lieutenant Dain.
- **Growth / Change:** TBD by the approved reveal scene.
- **Reveal(s):** Sera Dain was born Sera Vale; Kade Vale is her biological younger brother.
- **Cost:** TBD.
- **Mechanical Updates:** None.
- **Next Trigger (planned):** Approved manuscript scene that reveals the family connection.
