# Kade Vale

## Character Profile (Story-Facing)

### Quick Identity
- **Full Name:** Kade Vale
- **Known As:** Kade
- **Origin / District:** Outer Ring
- **Age:** Adult; exact age TBD
- **Role in Story:** Scavenger, field tactician, and core survivor of the early rift events

### Appearance
Kade has a lean, athletic build, warm tan-brown skin, brown eyes, short dark textured hair, and a visible scar along the right cheek. In the field he favors a hood, face covering, weathered cloak, scavenged armor pieces, layered dark clothing, gloves, utility pouches, worn boots, and a compact pack. His silhouette is intentionally low-profile and practical rather than military.

### Personality
- **Strengths:** Observant, decisive under pressure, resourceful, loyal once trust is earned
- **Flaws:** Guarded, risk-tolerant, controlling when afraid, quick to anticipate betrayal
- **Fears:** Being trapped without an exit; losing autonomy; failing someone he chose to protect
- **Desires:** Freedom of movement, control over his own life, and enough leverage to keep his people out of institutional control

### Background
Kade was born into the Vale family, the son of Darius Vale and Lysandra Vale and the younger brother of Sera Vale. During the Chapter 1 timeline he has not yet been born; Lysandra is approximately six months pregnant with him.

By the time Kade enters the main storyline, he has been shaped by Outer Ring survival culture: checkpoints, shortages, route knowledge, rival scavenger lanes, and an economy built around salvage, favors, and information. He learned to survive through stealth, preparation, and disciplined movement rather than formal military training.

His surname is not used publicly when he first enters the story. To Asher and the other early viewpoint characters, he is simply **Kade**. This prevents the audience from immediately connecting him to Lieutenant Dain.

### Motivations
- **Short-term:** Complete profitable scavenging runs, avoid unnecessary exposure, and keep his group alive
- **Long-term:** Build enough independence and leverage that neither he nor the people close to him can be easily controlled by the city

### Relationships
- **Asher Renn:** Acquaintance-turned-ally; Kade draws Asher into the scavenger lanes and early danger
- **John Renick:** Experienced fellow survivor; cooperation is practical, with underlying friction and mutual respect
- **Outer Ring Contacts:** Informal scavenger network; exact members TBD

### Arc
- **Start:** Competent scavenger who believes speed, preparation, and control can solve most problems
- **Pressure:** Rift events, forbidden sectors, and threats that cannot be managed through normal scavenger tactics
- **Change:** Learns that survival sometimes depends on trust, sacrifice, and accepting uncertainty
- **End state (planned):** TBD

### Secrets / Reveals
- **Hidden family connection:** Kade Vale is the biological brother of Sera Dain, born Sera Vale
- **Reveal control:** Early in the story, Kade is known only as **Kade**, while Sera is encountered by rank/title and adoptive surname as **Lieutenant Dain**. Their sibling relationship must not be revealed until the approved later story point.
- **Birth family:** Darius Vale and Lysandra Vale are his biological parents
- Kade knows more about certain scavenger routes and forbidden-sector boundaries than he openly shares
- The full reason for his resistance to sponsorship, testing, and institutional processing remains TBD
