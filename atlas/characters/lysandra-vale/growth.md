# Lysandra Vale — Growth Log

## Leveling Rule (Milestones) — Rule B
- **Level-Up Trigger:** major story beat involving survival, loss, family consequence, or decisive sacrifice
- **Update on level-up:** Class & Level, HP, Features/Traits, Skills, Equipment, and any material change in role
- **Ability Scores (ASI):** only at an approved milestone

## Baseline Record (Required)
- **Intro Story Point:** Chapter 1 — The Last Train East
- **Starting Level:** TBD
- **Starting Posting/Location:** Outer District, New Babylonia
- **Core Wound / Core Need:** She knows the city is unsafe for her children and needs to believe that leaving can create a different future
- **Core Lie / Core Belief:** No paper, official, or promise can be trusted more than her own judgment
- **Primary Bonds:** Darius Vale, Sera Vale, unborn Kade Vale, Mirella Renn, Asher Renn

## Timeline Entries

### Chapter/Batch: Chapter 1 — Departure and Eastgate Transit
- **Story Event Summary:**
  - Lysandra leaves New Babylonia with Darius, Sera, her unborn child, Mirella, and Asher.
  - The family passes Inspector Vey and boards Train Seven for Eastmere.
- **Growth / Change:** Her skepticism toward systems becomes the emotional counterweight to Darius's confidence in permits and preparation.
- **Reveal(s):** She is approximately six months pregnant with Kade Vale and has sacrificed an inherited silver comb to help fund passage.
- **Cost:** Family savings, heirloom loss, security, and certainty.
- **Mechanical Updates:** None.
- **Next Trigger (planned):** Eastgate rift attack.

### Chapter/Batch: Chapter 1 — Rift Attack and Service Trench
- **Story Event Summary:**
  - Lysandra helps move the family from Train Seven into the service trench.
  - When Darius refuses to abandon the jammed travel chest, she tells him that everything he loves is already in the tunnel.
  - A steam-pipe collapse separates the Vale family from Mirella and Asher.
- **Growth / Change:** Her established belief that people matter more than institutions or possessions becomes the practical rule that keeps the family moving.
- **Reveal(s):** Lysandra maintains clarity under extreme pressure despite pregnancy and physical strain.
- **Cost:** Most possessions are abandoned; Mirella and Asher are separated from the family.
- **Mechanical Updates:** None confirmed.
- **Next Trigger (planned):** Escape through the maintenance yard.

### Chapter/Batch: Chapter 1 — Maintenance Yard Escape
- **Story Event Summary:**
  - Lysandra reaches the maintenance yard with Darius and Sera.
  - She gets aboard the departing rail consist while Darius remains on the rear step looking for Mirella.
  - Sera briefly freezes while looking back toward the signal room before Lysandra pulls her toward the wagon.
- **Growth / Change:** The family survives the immediate station collapse, but safety now requires leaving friends behind without knowing their fate.
- **Reveal(s):** None beyond the consequences of separation.
- **Cost:** Emotional separation from Mirella and Asher; original resettlement plan destroyed.
- **Mechanical Updates:** None.
- **Next Trigger (planned):** Reach the East Marker and later endure the approved Sera-separation/adoption sequence.

### Planned Family Separation
- **Story Event Summary:** During the continuing evacuation crisis, Sera later becomes separated from the Vale family and is eventually adopted within the city.
- **Growth / Change:** TBD by approved manuscript development.
- **Reveal(s):** Sera Vale eventually becomes Sera Dain; her locket remains a physical link to her birth family.
- **Cost:** Separation from Sera and long-term fracture of the Vale family.
- **Mechanical Updates:** None.
- **Next Trigger (planned):** Approved scene establishing the separation and adoption chain.
