# John Renick — Growth Log

## Leveling Rule (Milestones) — Rule B
- **Level-Up Trigger:** major arc beat involving survival, revelation, loss, decisive choice, or transformation
- **Update on level-up:** Class & Level, Proficiency Bonus if changed, HP Max, Hit Dice, Features/Traits, Skills if affected, and Equipment if changed
- **Ability Scores (ASI):** only at ASI checkpoints or a major approved transformation

## Baseline Record (Required)
- **Intro Story Point:** Chapter 2 — scavenging sequence
- **Starting Level:** Rogue 2
- **Starting Posting/Location:** Outer Ring scavenger lanes
- **Core Wound / Core Need:** Years of survival have trained Renick to carry responsibility through experience and caution; he must learn where experience ends and shared trust begins
- **Core Lie / Core Belief:** "If I have seen enough and prepared enough, I can keep the people beside me from repeating the same mistakes."
- **Primary Bonds:** Asher Renn, Kade Dain, surviving scavenger group

## Timeline Entries

### Chapter/Batch: Chapter 2 — Scavenging Run
- **Story Event Summary:**
  - Renick joins the scavenging run as the most experienced survivor in the group.
  - Violence breaks the group apart until only Renick, Kade, and Asher remain together.
  - The surviving trio is forced toward forbidden-sector exposure and unfamiliar danger.
- **Growth / Change:** Renick moves from experienced observer to active protector as ordinary scavenger rules stop being enough.
- **Reveal(s):** His value to the group comes less from authority than from accumulated survival knowledge, patience, and judgment under pressure.
- **Cost:** Loss of members of the scavenging group and increasing responsibility for the younger survivors beside him.
- **Mechanical Updates:** None confirmed.
- **Next Trigger (planned):** A rift or Wraith encounter forces Renick to confront a threat that cannot be solved by experience alone.
