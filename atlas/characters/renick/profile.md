# John Renick

## Character Profile (Story-Facing)

### Quick Identity
- **Full Name:** John Renick
- **Known As:** Renick
- **Origin / District:** Outer Ring
- **Age:** Approximately 40
- **Role in Story:** Seasoned scavenger, guide, mentor, and stabilizing survivor within the group

### Appearance
Renick is a dark-skinned man of approximately forty with a solid, strong build and an experienced physical presence. He has brown eyes, short textured black hair carrying visible grey, and a full salt-and-pepper beard. Facial scars mark the right side of his face. His clothing is practical Outer Ring scavenger gear: weathered layered fabrics, scarf or neck wraps, patched protective pieces, gloves, rugged boots, a utility pack, and equipment chosen for endurance rather than appearance.

### Personality
- **Strengths:** Experienced, pragmatic, protective, observant, calm under pressure, resourceful
- **Flaws:** Blunt, slow to trust authority, accustomed to carrying responsibility alone, can become overly certain of his own judgment
- **Fears:** Losing people to mistakes he believes should have been preventable; seeing younger survivors repeat failures he has already lived through
- **Desires:** Keep people alive, make hard-earned experience useful, and preserve enough independence to choose his own path

### Background
Renick has spent much of his life surviving in the Outer Ring and learned early that information, observation, and adaptability are often more valuable than force. Years of scavenging gave him deep practical knowledge of abandoned infrastructure, unsafe routes, salvage work, and the behavior of people under pressure. He distrusts authority and sponsorship systems and has learned to judge people more by what they do than by what they promise.

### Motivations
- **Short-term:** Guide the surviving group through dangerous territory and reduce avoidable risk
- **Long-term:** Ensure that the experience he has accumulated serves something more meaningful than simply surviving one more day

### Relationships
- **Asher Renn:** Mutual-survival bond; Renick recognizes Asher's steadiness and increasingly treats his survival as a personal responsibility
- **Kade Dain:** Fellow scavenger and survivor; capable cooperation complicated by different instincts about risk and control
- **The Group:** Renick serves as a stabilizing presence and practical mentor when panic or inexperience threatens decision-making

### Arc
- **Start:** Veteran survivor who believes experience, observation, and preparation can keep danger manageable
- **Pressure:** Rift events, forbidden-sector exposure, and threats that exceed anything normal scavenging experience prepared him for
- **Change:** Forced to accept that experience alone is not enough and that survival may require trust, adaptation, and shared responsibility
- **End state (planned):** TBD

- TBD; no hidden supernatural or possession outcome is current canon
