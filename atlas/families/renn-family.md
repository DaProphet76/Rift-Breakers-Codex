# Renn Family

## Overview
The Renn family household introduced in Chapter 1 consists of Mirella Renn and her three-year-old son, Asher Renn. Asher's father is already dead before the chapter begins. Mirella and Asher attempt to leave New Babylonia with the Vale family.

## Structure
- **Mirella Renn:** Mother; seamstress; primary caregiver and protector of Asher.
- **Asher Renn:** Son; age three during Chapter 1.
- **Asher's father:** Deceased before Chapter 1; name and history not yet established.
- **Mirella's mother:** Deceased or absent from the current household; former owner of Mirella's pendant. Name and history not yet established.

## Goals
- Leave New Babylonia's Outer Districts.
- Reach Eastmere with the Vale family.
- Protect Asher from the violence and warning-driven childhood of the city.
- Preserve the few meaningful family objects Mirella has chosen not to sell or abandon.

## Methods
- Mirella supports the household through seamstress work.
- The family travels lightly because Mirella expects to carry Asher herself.
- Mirella relies on practical observation, knowledge gathered through her work, and decisive action during danger.
- Darius Vale helps secure the family's travel permits.

## Relationships
- **Vale Family:** Close friends and travelling companions; Darius has known Mirella for nine years by Chapter 1.
- **New Babylonia authorities:** Necessary for departure but viewed with distrust.
- **Unidentified Frost-Wreathed Entity:** Becomes directly connected to Mirella and Asher during the Eastgate attack; exact nature of the connection remains unknown.

## Notable Members
- Mirella Renn
- Asher Renn

## Canon Notes
- **Confirmed:** Mirella is Asher's mother; Asher is three during Chapter 1; Asher's father is dead; Mirella is a seamstress; the household lives in the Outer Districts.
- **Confirmed family heirloom:** Mirella's pendant belonged to her mother and is transferred to Asher during the Eastgate attack.
- **Confirmed emotional object:** Asher's red wooden "horse" remains with him throughout the escape.
- **Intentionally unknown:** Asher's father's name and death circumstances; Mirella's mother's name; deeper Renn lineage; meaning of the pendant.
- **Open questions:** Mirella's final fate after leading the frost entity away; how Asher is recovered after the Chapter 1 ending.
