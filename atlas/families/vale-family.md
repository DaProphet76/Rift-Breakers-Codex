# Vale Family

## Overview
The Vale family is an Outer District household introduced in Chapter 1. Darius Vale and Lysandra Vale attempt to leave New Babylonia with their young daughter Sera and Lysandra's unborn child, later established in hidden canon as Kade Vale.

## Structure
- **Darius Vale:** Father; millwright; organizes and finances the family's attempted escape.
- **Lysandra Vale:** Mother; approximately six months pregnant during Chapter 1.
- **Sera Vale:** Daughter and child during Chapter 1; later known as Sera Dain after separation and adoption.
- **Kade Vale:** Unborn during Chapter 1; biological son of Darius and Lysandra and younger brother of Sera.

## Goals
- Leave New Babylonia's Outer Districts.
- Reach Eastmere through the resettlement route.
- Keep the children alive and together.
- Build a safer life beyond the city's unequal protections.

## Methods
- Years of saving and sacrifice to obtain legal travel permits.
- Reliance on Darius's trade status and labour exemptions.
- Practical cooperation within the household.
- Mutual support with the Renn family during travel and crisis.

## Relationships
- **Renn Family:** Close family friends/travelling companions; Darius also secures permits for Mirella and Asher.
- **New Babylonia authorities:** Necessary but distrusted gatekeepers controlling departure.
- **Sera's later adoptive family:** Future relationship associated with the surname Dain; exact adoptive parent/household remains to be established.

## Notable Members
- Darius Vale
- Lysandra Vale
- Sera Vale / Sera Dain
- Kade Vale

## Canon Notes
- **Confirmed in Chapter 1:** Darius and Lysandra are married; Sera is their daughter; Lysandra is six months pregnant; the family attempts to reach Eastmere.
- **Approved hidden canon:** The unborn child is Kade Vale. Sera and Kade are biological siblings.
- **Approved future continuity:** Sera becomes separated from her birth family during the escape period and is later adopted, taking the surname Dain.
- **Revelation control:** Adult Sera's birth identity as Sera Vale and her sibling relationship to Kade Vale are later reveals and must not be exposed prematurely in reader-facing prose.
- **Open questions:** Exact outcome of Darius, Lysandra, and young Sera immediately after the Eastgate escape; identity of Sera's adoptive parent/household.
