# Unidentified Frost-Wreathed Entity

## Summary
An unnamed, highly dangerous humanoid entity crosses the Eastgate rift after the Rakers during Chapter 1. Unlike the Rakers, it moves with deliberate purpose, commands or suppresses them through simple gestures, and appears to be searching for a specific target associated in some way with Mirella Renn's pendant.

## Classification
- **Type:** Unidentified humanoid hostile entity
- **Realm of origin:** Unknown; enters through the Eastgate rift from the storm-dark environment visible beyond it
- **Working label:** "Unidentified Frost-Wreathed Entity" is a descriptive Atlas label, not an in-world confirmed name

## Description
- Taller than any human man present on the platform
- Extremely narrow humanoid silhouette
- Tattered darkness hangs from the shoulders like smoke-drowned robes
- Pale armour covers the body in uneven plates
- Armour rims are coated with white frost
- Face remains hidden inside a deep hood
- Two faint winter-blue points of light are visible within the hood
- Movement is slow, measured, and controlled rather than animalistic

## Intelligence and Communication
- Demonstrates clear target selection and situational awareness
- Searches carriage by carriage instead of attacking randomly
- Uses hand gestures that cause nearby Rakers to halt, bow, crouch, or move away
- No spoken language is observed
- Appears capable of distinguishing between threats and its primary objective

## Abilities
- Generates intense cold around its body and footsteps
- Frost spreads rapidly across stone, rails, windows, weapons, and nearby surfaces
- Can extinguish nearby fire through sudden cold
- Can freeze rifles to a wielder's hands and rapidly frost human bodies
- Can project or cause a wave of cold over distance
- Can cause frost/ice to rise from the pavement as a striking formation
- Appears able to restrain or command Rakers without physical contact
- Continues functioning after sustained rifle damage
- Fresh frost accumulates over cracked armour after damage; whether this is true regeneration is not yet confirmed

## Weaknesses / Limitations
- Conventional gunfire can damage the armour
- Concentrated rifle fire cracks plates, strips material from its body covering, and impairs one arm
- It is not invulnerable
- Exact kill conditions and resistance limits are unknown

## Ecology / Role in World
Its relationship to the Rakers is hierarchical or authoritative rather than merely coincidental. Rakers flatten themselves, withdraw from its path, and obey its raised hand. The broader species, rank, faction, or origin of the entity is unknown.

## Encounters
- **Chapter 1 — Eastgate / Train Seven Rift Event:** Crosses after the Rakers, kills multiple guards and soldiers through extreme cold, searches the station and maintenance yard, responds to the activation/transfer of Mirella's pendant, enters the repair shed, and follows Mirella away from Asher at the chapter's end.

## Canon Notes
- **Confirmed:** Humanoid; taller than a man; hooded; pale plated armour; winter-blue lights within hood; frost aura; ranged freezing effect; control/influence over Rakers; vulnerable to concentrated conventional fire.
- **Strongly suggested but not confirmed:** It is searching for the pendant, Asher, Mirella, or something connected to them.
- **Intentionally unknown:** True name; species; rank; motives; language; realm; whether frost is innate, technological, magical, or anomalous; connection to the pendant.
- **Open questions:** Why it follows Mirella; why the pendant reacts; whether it opened the rift or only crossed it; whether similar entities exist.
