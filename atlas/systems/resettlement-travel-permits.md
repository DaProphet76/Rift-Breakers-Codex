# Resettlement and Travel Permit System

## Summary
New Babylonia restricts movement through a permit system that controls passage through checkpoints and onto outbound rail transport. Chapter 1 shows Darius Vale using authorized resettlement papers to move his household, Mirella Renn, and Asher Renn toward Eastmere.

## Canon Notes
- **Confirmed document categories in Chapter 1:** adult passages, child passage, dependent mark for an unborn child, named passages for Mirella Renn and Asher Renn, and labour exemptions.
- **Confirmed process:** inspectors review seals, issue dates, destination, purpose, occupation, dependent status, and child identity before stamping the papers.
- **Confirmed limit:** permits expire if the gate seals before the authorized departure.
- **Confirmed visual handling:** the papers are carried folded in waxed cloth and receive an inspector's stamp at Eastgate.
- **Intentionally unknown:** exact issuing agency, paper size, seal design, official fee structure, fraud prevention, appeal process, and whether bribery is formally embedded or merely feared/common.

## Details
- Darius's permits are issued one day before travel even though normal travel allocation closed five days earlier.
- The family travels under labour exemptions.
- Destination is recorded as Eastmere and purpose as resettlement.
- Occupations matter during inspection: Darius identifies himself as a millwright; Mirella as a seamstress.
- Darius's acquisition of the permits costs eight years of savings, Lysandra's inherited silver comb, and a future favour. Current canon does not establish how much of that cost was official, unofficial, or mediated through third parties.
- An eastern rail mark on a travel chest visibly signals outbound travel and attracts attention in the Outer District.

## Cultural Impact
- Travel documents are valuable enough to reshape a family's finances and possessions.
- Residents do not assume that valid documents guarantee humane treatment or passage.
- Permits represent both hope of escape and dependence on institutional approval.
- The system reinforces class and mobility differences between residents who can leave and those who cannot.

## Known Conflicts / Tensions
- Valid permits can still be rendered useless by gate closure.
- Residents fear bribery and arbitrary enforcement.
- Labour exemptions can override normal allocation timing, creating unequal access.
- The system promises resettlement but does not guarantee the destination or route remains safe.
