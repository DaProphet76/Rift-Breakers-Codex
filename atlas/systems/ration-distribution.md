# Ration Distribution

## Summary
New Babylonia's Outer Districts use public ration pumps or ration-distribution points as part of ordinary daily life. Chapter 1 establishes residents carrying pails to these pumps in the morning, indicating a controlled distribution system for at least one essential resource.

## Canon Notes
- **Confirmed:** Public ration pumps exist in the Outer Districts; residents carry pails to them as part of routine daily activity.
- **Not established:** What substance is dispensed, whether the pumps distribute water or another ration, how allocation is measured, who operates them, and how eligibility is tracked.
- **Intentionally unknown:** Schedule, ration size, technology, enforcement, pricing, and relationship to household status or labour assignments.

## Details
- Ration-pump traffic appears alongside workers heading to workshops, suggesting the system is integrated into ordinary morning routines.
- The existence of ration pumps reinforces Chapter 1's depiction of scarcity and managed access to basic resources.

## Cultural Impact
- Residents organize part of daily life around controlled access to essential supplies.
- Pails and queues should be considered part of the everyday visual language of the Outer Districts when relevant.

## Known Conflicts / Tensions
- The chapter does not establish shortages at the pumps themselves, only that controlled distribution exists.
- Do not assume the pumps provide drinking water until a later source confirms it.
