# Rifts

## Summary
Rifts are tears or openings between the normal world and another realm or space. The prologue establishes them as a recurring post-war threat, while Chapter 1 provides the first detailed current-manuscript visual of a rift forming at Eastgate Station and allowing hostile entities to cross through.

## Definitions
- **Rift:** A tear/opening in the world through which hostile entities can cross.
- **Gateway:** Chapter 1's visual form of an active rift after the initial crack rises into a vertical arch/doorway.
- **Other side:** The visible environment beyond the Eastgate gateway; its formal realm name is not yet established. It is tracked provisionally as `atlas/locations/unidentified-eastgate-rift-realm.md`.

## Rules / Constraints
- A rift can begin as a luminous crack across solid ground.
- The Eastgate rift forms a curved blue-white arc that lifts upright into a standing doorway.
- Physical entities can cross the threshold from the other side into New Babylonia.
- The Eastgate rift expands during the event, eventually filling the road beneath the Eastern Wall.
- Current canon does not yet establish how rifts are created, closed, predicted, or controlled.
- Current canon does not establish whether all rifts follow the exact same formation sequence.

## Observable Effects
- Black crack across pavement with blue-white light bleeding from the edges
- Repeated impacts/thumps beneath the station before full formation
- Pale branching energy snapping around the rim and striking nearby stone
- Visible alternate environment inside the opening: storm-dark clouds, drifting ash, and distant orange fire
- Station lamps flicker and Train Seven's engine loses rhythm immediately before the opening; causation is not yet confirmed
- Hostile creatures gather visibly on the far side before crossing
- The active gateway pulses and expands

## Failure Modes
- Rail lines and paving can be physically severed by the forming crack
- A rift can create immediate creature incursions in crowded civilian spaces
- Defensive walls and checkpoints provide little protection if the opening forms inside or across them
- Infrastructure failures may accompany manifestation, but the mechanism is unknown

## Institutional Use / Exploitation
No controlled institutional use is established in Chapter 1. The prologue indicates humanity has learned to fight creatures that emerge from rifts, but no current Chapter 1 source establishes reliable containment, detection, or exploitation technology.

## Canon Notes
- **Confirmed by prologue:** Rifts appeared after the wars; hostile beings cross where the world is thin; such incursions helped drive populations inward.
- **Confirmed by Chapter 1:** A rift can manifest as a standing gateway; Rakers and a larger frost-associated entity cross through it; the gateway remains active through the observed sequence.
- **Intentionally unknown:** Origin, energy source, realm structure, trigger conditions, closure rules, frequency, and whether different rift classes exist.
- **Open questions:** Whether the Eastgate power/engine disturbances are caused by the rift; whether the unidentified frost entity deliberately opened or merely used the gateway.
