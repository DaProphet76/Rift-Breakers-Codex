# Eastgate Train Seven Rift Event

## Summary
The Eastgate Train Seven Rift Event is the catastrophic Chapter 1 incident in which a rift opens at Eastgate Station during an attempted resettlement departure. Rakers and a larger frost-associated entity cross through, causing mass casualties, infrastructure collapse, and the separation of the Vale and Renn families.

## Date / Era
- Exact date: TBD
- Story placement: Book One, Chapter 1 — `The Last Train East`
- Time of day: Around the first and second noon bells

## What Happened
- Train Seven prepares to depart Eastgate Station for Eastmere.
- Station lamps flicker, the engine falters, and impacts are felt beneath the station.
- A black crack opens across the pavement and rail, glowing blue-white.
- The crack curves upward into a standing gateway showing a storm-dark environment beyond.
- Rakers cross first and attack passengers, guards, railway workers, and infrastructure.
- A Raker collision contributes to the bell tower collapsing across the station and Train Seven's forward track.
- A taller frost-wreathed entity crosses after the Rakers, kills guards through extreme cold, and searches through the station rather than attacking randomly.
- Inspector Vey orders the Eastern Wall gate sealed.
- Darius, Lysandra, Sera, Mirella, and Asher escape Train Seven and enter the service trench.
- The group is split at a trench junction after Rakers breach the passage and a steam pipe collapses across the fork.
- Darius, Lysandra, and Sera reach the maintenance yard and escape aboard a small rail engine/wagon formation.
- Mirella and Asher emerge separately through the signal room and repair shed.
- Mirella hides Asher in a maintenance carriage, gives him her pendant, and deliberately draws the frost entity away.
- Chapter 1 ends with Asher concealed while Mirella leads the entity into the ruined yard.

## Consequences
- Train Seven fails to depart by its intended route.
- Eastgate Station suffers severe structural and operational damage.
- Multiple guards, soldiers, civilians, and workers are killed or injured.
- The Eastern Wall fails to prevent an internal rift incursion.
- The Vale and Renn families are separated.
- Asher becomes the holder of Mirella's pendant.
- The fate of Mirella and the immediate reunion attempt at the East Marker remain unresolved.

## What People Are Taught
Not yet established. Chapter 1 shows the event directly but does not reveal how New Babylonia later records, suppresses, explains, or publicizes it.

## Canon Notes
- **Confirmed:** Rift manifestation, Raker incursion, frost entity, station damage, family separation, Asher receiving the pendant.
- **Intentionally unknown:** Official casualty count; official explanation; whether the event is publicly acknowledged; whether the rift closes; Mirella's fate; Vey's final outcome; full station aftermath.
- **Open questions:** How the event enters public history, whether it is linked to later rift activity, and whether the frost entity's target was Asher, Mirella, or the pendant.
