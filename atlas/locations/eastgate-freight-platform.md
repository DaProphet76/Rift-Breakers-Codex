# Eastgate Freight Platform

## Summary
The Eastgate Freight Platform is the boarding area assigned to Train Seven and the primary surface action location during the first phase of the Chapter 1 rift attack.

## Description
Train Seven waits on the last platform behind a squat iron engine. Porters move sacks, tool crates, cages of chickens, and passenger possessions. The platform is surrounded by rails, baggage carts, freight wagons, station lamps, the bell tower, and the nearby gatehouse.

The rift begins as a black crack across pavement beyond the freight ramp, cuts through one rail, then rises into a blue-white standing gateway. Rakers emerge directly into the crowded station environment.

## Environment / Conditions
- Coal smoke, steam, soot, damp wool, rain, and heavy passenger traffic
- Freight ramp and baggage carts
- Blue station lamps
- Multiple tracks and rail-side loading activity
- Bell tower adjacent enough that its collapse strikes Train Seven
- During the attack: shattered windows, debris, frost, rifle fire, fire, and creature movement across roofs/posts/cars

## Population / Control
- Resettlement passengers
- Porters and railway workers
- Armed guards
- Pell and Train Seven crew
- Inspector Vey and checkpoint/security personnel nearby

## Risks
- Dense civilian crowd in an exposed rail environment
- Rift formation directly across the rail/pavement
- Rakers can climb train walls, roofs, posts, and wagons
- Bell-tower collapse blocks the forward track and damages the train
- Gate closure can trap survivors inside the station complex

## Narrative Use
- Main visual arena for the first large-scale creature reveal
- Provides the audience's first full view of a Raker
- Establishes the frost entity immediately after the Raker incursion
- Transforms an ordinary departure platform into a mass-casualty battle space

## Canon Notes
- **Confirmed:** Freight ramp; Train Seven on last platform; baggage carts; freight handling; nearby bell tower; rail severed by rift crack; platform is the initial Raker attack zone.
- **Intentionally unknown:** Exact platform dimensions, numbering system, canopy/roof layout, signage, and full track plan.
- **Open questions:** Final post-attack condition and whether the platform is later rebuilt or abandoned.
