# Eastgate Repair Shed

## Summary
The Eastgate Repair Shed is the final interior location in Chapter 1. Mirella carries Asher into it from the signal room and uses a partially dismantled maintenance carriage to hide him from the unidentified frost-wreathed entity.

## Description
The shed is long and dim, crowded with broken rail equipment. Chains hang from ceiling beams. Loose railway wheels stand taller than three-year-old Asher. A small maintenance carriage sits inside with peeling paint and one side opened for repairs. A repair shelf holds a signal flare that Mirella later ignites.

## Environment / Conditions
- Long, dim industrial interior
- Broken rail equipment stored throughout
- Hanging chains from overhead beams
- Loose wheels and maintenance hardware
- Rails running through the shed
- Oil, old wood, and cold iron smells around the maintenance carriage
- During the final sequence, frost creeps across rails, floor, chains, and grease as the entity approaches

## Population / Control
- No regular repair crew is present during the Chapter 1 crisis
- Designed for railway maintenance and narrow service-line equipment
- Directly connected to the signal room and opens toward the maintenance yard

## Risks
- Cluttered movement paths
- Limited concealment despite heavy equipment
- Rakers enter behind the larger entity
- Extreme cold spreads through the building
- The frost entity can command the Rakers to remain still while it focuses on Mirella/Asher

## Narrative Use
- Location of Asher's hiding place and Mirella's final Chapter 1 decision
- Introduces the maintenance carriage, signal flare, pendant transfer, and deliberate diversion
- Key visual set for animation because it combines intimate mother/child action with large industrial scale and encroaching frost

## Canon Notes
- **Confirmed:** Long/dim; broken rail equipment; hanging chains; loose large wheels; repair shelf; maintenance carriage; direct access from signal room; exterior doors toward yard.
- **Intentionally unknown:** Shed dimensions, exterior façade, normal staff, precise machinery inventory, and post-event condition.
- **Open questions:** Whether Asher remains hidden there after the chapter endpoint and who eventually finds him.
