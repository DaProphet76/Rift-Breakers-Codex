# Eastern Settlements

## Summary
The Eastern Settlements are communities outside or beyond New Babylonia's eastern boundary that residents associate with resettlement and the possibility of a safer life. Eastmere is the only settlement named directly in Chapter 1.

## Description
Chapter 1 does not provide a direct visual of the region. Darius and the others have heard claims of open fields, clean wells, safer conditions, and more honest officials, but the narration explicitly frames much of this as hearsay rather than confirmed fact.

## Environment / Conditions
- Located east of New Babylonia
- Connected to the city by rail and road routes
- Eastmere is associated with proximity to the sea
- Open fields and clean wells are reported but not verified in Chapter 1

## Population / Control
- Civilian settlements capable of receiving resettlement traffic
- Ordinary trades such as seamstress work exist in Eastmere
- Exact governments, populations, military protection, and relationship to New Babylonia are unknown

## Risks
- Rail lines may be damaged or absent
- Safety claims may be exaggerated or false
- Raker presence is not reliably established
- Travel from New Babylonia depends on controlled gates, permits, and functioning transport

## Narrative Use
- Represents the world beyond New Babylonia and the hope driving the Chapter 1 migration attempt
- Provides a future geographic category for additional towns and communities as the story expands eastward

## Canon Notes
- **Confirmed:** Eastern settlements exist beyond New Babylonia; Eastmere is one; rail resettlement routes serve them.
- **Reported but unverified:** Clean wells, open fields, honest officials, and greater safety.
- **Intentionally unknown:** Number of settlements, borders, regional government, terrain, economy, and actual security conditions.
- **Open questions:** How independent these settlements are from New Babylonia and what lies between them.
