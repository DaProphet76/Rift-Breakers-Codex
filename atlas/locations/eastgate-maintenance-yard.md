# Eastgate Maintenance Yard

## Summary
The Eastgate Maintenance Yard lies east of the Eastern Wall and forms the final large exterior action space in Chapter 1. It contains service tracks, rail equipment, the signal-room frontage, and the route used by a small evacuation rail consist carrying Darius, Lysandra, and Sera away from the station.

## Description
The yard is visible through three broad signal-room windows. A small rail engine waits on an outer track with two freight cars and an open passenger wagon. Porters and guards force survivors aboard while Rakers move through the area. The active rift expands until it fills the road beneath the Eastern Wall, allowing more Rakers into the yard.

## Environment / Conditions
- Open rail-service area beyond the wall
- Multiple service/outer tracks
- Rail equipment, wagons, baggage, and maintenance structures
- Signal room and repair shed border the yard
- During the attack: bodies, smoke, gunfire, frost, damaged equipment, and fleeing survivors dominate the space
- The unidentified frost entity crosses the yard slowly while searching

## Population / Control
- Porters, guards, soldiers, railway workers, and survivors are present during the evacuation
- A driver operates the small evacuation engine
- No named permanent supervisor or formal yard authority is established

## Risks
- Raker incursion across open ground and rail equipment
- Expanding rift/gateway
- Extreme cold generated by the frost entity
- Scattered defensive forces unable to concentrate fire
- Limited safe routes once the station and main track are compromised

## Narrative Use
- Provides the visual separation between Mirella/Asher and the escaping Vale family
- Demonstrates the scale of the Eastgate disaster beyond Train Seven itself
- Serves as the area Mirella enters when deliberately drawing the frost entity away from Asher

## Canon Notes
- **Confirmed:** Located east of the wall; visible from signal room; outer track; small engine, two freight cars, open passenger wagon; active evacuation; repair shed nearby.
- **Intentionally unknown:** Exact dimensions, track layout, number of sheds, normal daily operations, and final post-attack state.
- **Open questions:** Where the evacuation consist travels after leaving the yard and how long the yard remains overrun.
