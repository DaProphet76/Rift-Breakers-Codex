# East Marker

## Summary
The East Marker is a named rendezvous point outside the immediate Eastgate disaster area. Darius shouts to Mirella that his group will wait there after the two families are separated during the Chapter 1 escape.

## Description
No physical description is yet established. Its name and Darius's use of it indicate a recognizable waypoint associated with the eastern route beyond Eastgate.

## Environment / Conditions
- Located somewhere east of the station/maintenance-yard escape route
- Likely connected to the eastern road or rail corridor, but the exact placement is not confirmed
- Current visual details are TBD

## Population / Control
- No permanent population or controlling authority established
- Recognizable enough that Darius expects Mirella to understand the reference immediately

## Risks
- Chapter 1 ends before Mirella can attempt to reach it
- Its condition after the rift attack is unknown

## Narrative Use
- Serves as the intended reunion point after Darius, Lysandra, and Sera are separated from Mirella and Asher
- Important continuity anchor for any subsequent account of the Vale family's escape and Mirella/Asher's immediate aftermath

## Canon Notes
- **Confirmed:** Named eastern waypoint; chosen by Darius as a reunion location.
- **Intentionally unknown:** Exact distance, appearance, construction, ownership, and whether the group successfully reaches or waits there.
- **Open questions:** Whether it is a rail marker, road marker, border marker, or broader local landmark.
