# Eastgate Signal Room

## Summary
The Eastgate Signal Room is an elevated or raised rail-control space reached by Mirella and Asher after they are separated from the Vale family in the service trench. It overlooks the eastern maintenance yard and becomes Mirella's vantage point for seeing Darius, Lysandra, and Sera escape.

## Description
The room contains rows of coloured control levers, a wall map, and three broad windows overlooking the maintenance yard east of the Eastern Wall. A swollen door connects to the rising trench passage, while a second door leads into the old repair shed.

## Environment / Conditions
- Rail-control equipment arranged in rows
- Coloured mechanical levers
- Wall-mounted route/map display
- Three broad windows facing the maintenance yard
- Connected directly to the repair shed
- During the attack, the room becomes cold as the frost-wreathed entity approaches

## Population / Control
- Normal operating staff are not shown during the Chapter 1 sequence
- Intended function is railway signaling/control, but exact staffing and authority are not established

## Risks
- Limited exits
- Large windows expose occupants visually to the maintenance yard
- The frost entity identifies Mirella's position through or toward the room
- Door frames can swell or jam, delaying escape

## Narrative Use
- Lets Mirella see the Vale family escaping while she and Asher remain trapped behind
- Provides the transition from subterranean industrial space into the maintenance-yard/rear-station environment
- Serves as the route into the repair shed where Asher is hidden

## Canon Notes
- **Confirmed:** Coloured levers; wall map; three broad windows; view over maintenance yard; connection to service trench and repair shed.
- **Intentionally unknown:** Exact elevation, signaling technology, normal crew size, full lever functions, room dimensions, and construction date.
- **Open questions:** Whether the room remains operational after the Eastgate event.
