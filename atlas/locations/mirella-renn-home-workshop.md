# Mirella Renn Home and Workshop

## Summary
Mirella Renn's home doubles as her seamstress workspace and is the second major domestic location in Chapter 1. It is where Darius and Lysandra collect Mirella and three-year-old Asher before the group leaves for Eastgate Station.

## Description
The house stands three lanes north of the Vale home and leans toward its neighboring building. Its lower walls are old fitted stone, while the upper construction is a patchwork of timber and sheet metal. Washed cloth hangs across the front room and filters the windows into pale blue and green light.

Inside, Mirella works at a table surrounded by heaps of mended coats and trousers. Asher plays beneath the table with six painted wooden animals.

## Environment / Conditions
- Mixed old-stone and improvised upper construction
- Dense Outer District neighborhood
- Work and domestic life share the same interior space
- Front-room light is filtered through hanging washed cloth
- Clothing repair, parcels, sewing tools, and practical household items define the workspace

## Population / Control
- Occupied by Mirella Renn and Asher Renn at the start of Chapter 1
- Functions as Mirella's seamstress workplace
- Exact ownership or tenancy status is not established

## Risks
- Thin-walled neighborhood where residents avoid openly criticizing officials
- Limited ability to carry possessions during evacuation
- Home is abandoned when Mirella and Asher depart for Eastgate

## Narrative Use
- Establishes Mirella as a working seamstress and practical parent
- Provides Asher's childhood visual baseline before the disaster
- Introduces the red wooden horse and Mirella's pendant
- Should visually distinguish Mirella's household through textile work, filtered cloth colours, and the blend of old masonry with improvised construction

## Canon Notes
- **Confirmed:** Three lanes north of the Vale home; leaning structure; fitted-stone lower walls; timber/sheet-metal upper construction; washed cloth across front room; worktable; mended clothing; Asher's wooden animals.
- **Intentionally unknown:** Number of rooms, rear layout, sleeping arrangements, roof form, exact exterior colour, ownership, and post-departure fate.
- **Open questions:** Whether the home is revisited or survives later city events.
