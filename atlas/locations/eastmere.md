# Eastmere

## Summary
Eastmere is the eastern settlement named as the destination of the Vale and Renn families in Chapter 1. It is presented to residents of New Babylonia as a possible place of resettlement and greater safety, but most of what the characters know about it is rumor, promise, or second-hand information.

## Description
Eastmere is described as a town rather than a city. Darius says the sea cannot be seen from the town, while Mirella has said it can be smelled there. The rail journey from Eastgate is estimated by Pell at two days if the line is clear and four days if conditions are poor.

## Environment / Conditions
- Near enough to the sea for its presence to be noticeable, according to Mirella
- Said to lie among eastern settlements with more open land than New Babylonia
- Exact terrain, architecture, climate, coastline distance, and settlement layout are not yet established

## Population / Control
- Has ordinary civilian trades such as seamstresses
- Resettlement traffic is directed there from New Babylonia
- Government, population size, defenses, and relationship to New Babylonia remain undefined

## Risks
- The route may be unreliable or damaged; Pell explicitly treats line failure as a realistic possibility
- Claims of safety are not verified in Chapter 1
- Darius's reassurance that Rakers avoid the sea is not established as fact

## Narrative Use
- Represents the hoped-for alternative to life in New Babylonia's Outer Districts
- Functions as the destination the families sacrifice nearly everything to reach
- Provides a future visual contrast point if/when the settlement is shown directly

## Canon Notes
- **Confirmed:** Named town/settlement east of New Babylonia; rail destination; roughly two days away under clear rail conditions; associated with resettlement.
- **Reported but unverified:** Cleaner wells, open fields, safer conditions, honest officials, and reduced Raker danger.
- **Intentionally unknown:** Exact appearance, government, population, defenses, distance from the sea, and actual safety.
- **Open questions:** Whether Eastmere fulfills any of the promises attached to it and whether the Vale family reaches it.
