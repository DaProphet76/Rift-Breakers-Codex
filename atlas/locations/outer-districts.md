# Outer Districts

## Summary
The Outer Districts, also described in project material as the outer rings, are the poorer and less protected portions of New Babylonia's urban system. Chapter 1 establishes the eastern outer district as the home area of Darius Vale, Lysandra Vale, Mirella Renn, Sera, and young Asher Renn.

## Description
The district is built from layers of survival and reuse. Patched houses line narrow streets. Some homes combine old fitted stone with later timber and sheet-metal additions. Workshops, ration pumps, laundry lines, crooked chimneys, rusted roofs, and repaired domestic goods are common.

The oldest areas contain visible remains from before the Sundering: smooth black pillars, broken stone figures, ruined upper floors, and structures reused as foundations for shops and homes.

## Environment / Conditions
- Narrow streets and dense housing
- Fog, soot, coal smoke, damp weather, and industrial noise
- Thin walls and limited privacy
- Repaired and repurposed construction rather than uniform architecture
- Workshop labour and ration collection are part of daily life
- Warning bells and checkpoint signals are familiar enough to shape childhood behavior

## Population / Control
- Working families, tradespeople, labourers, vendors, and children
- Residents depend on city ration and labour systems
- Movement toward protected or external areas is controlled through checkpoints and permits
- The eastern wall protects the richer interior more reliably than it protects people living in its shadow

## Risks
- Poverty and scarcity
- Exposure to official scrutiny and arbitrary enforcement
- Limited physical protection compared with richer rings
- Rift and creature threats
- Residents can be caught outside defensive walls when gates seal

## Narrative Use
- Establishes the baseline visual and social world that shaped the principal cast
- Provides contrast with the protected inner rings and the promised safety of eastern settlements
- Should visually communicate endurance, repair, adaptation, and inequality rather than total collapse

## Canon Notes
- **Confirmed:** Patched housing; ration pumps; workshops; narrow streets; pre-Sundering ruins; eastern wall shadow; lower protection than richer rings.
- **Terminology:** Current sources use outer district, outer districts, outer ring, and outer rings. These are treated as related terms pending a finalized city map and formal naming system.
- **Intentionally unknown:** Exact district boundaries, numbering, population, and administrative subdivisions.
- **Open questions:** Whether "fringe" is an official geographic label or informal resident terminology.
