# Eastgate Service Trench

## Summary
The Eastgate Service Trench is a narrow maintenance passage running beneath the station platform and Eastern Wall toward the eastern maintenance yard. Mirella uses it as the family's emergency escape route during the Chapter 1 rift attack.

## Description
The trench is scarcely wide enough for two people. Pipes crowd both walls and sweat hot water. Red lamps glow at long intervals. The air tastes of rust and coal smoke. A locked hatch on the station side provides access, and Inspector Vey's key ring contains the key needed to open it.

Farther east, the passage forks. A blue arrow points right while the left passage slopes upward toward daylight and the signal-room area.

## Environment / Conditions
- Confined subterranean industrial passage
- Hot-water pipes along the walls
- Red utility lamps at intervals
- Rust, coal smoke, steam, and limited visibility
- Narrow enough that debris or creatures can easily block movement
- A pipe rupture during the attack fills the junction with white steam

## Population / Control
- Used by maintenance/trench crews during normal operations
- Access is controlled by a locked hatch and official keys
- Mirella knows the route because she repaired uniforms for trench crews and overheard their conversations

## Risks
- Extreme confinement during evacuation
- Hot pipes and steam ruptures
- Rakers can breach the hatch and enter the passage
- Pipe failures and falling debris can split groups and block return routes
- Limited exits make it a dangerous chokepoint

## Narrative Use
- Major Chapter 1 escape environment
- Provides the physical location where the Vale and Renn families are separated
- Useful visual reference for tight, industrial, red-lit subterranean sequences

## Canon Notes
- **Confirmed:** Runs beneath the platform and Eastern Wall; narrow; pipe-lined; hot water; red lamps; locked access; fork with blue right-pointing arrow; left passage rises toward daylight/signal room.
- **Intentionally unknown:** Full network map, trench length, maintenance schedule, exact construction materials, and whether it connects to other city districts.
- **Open questions:** Whether this trench is part of a larger citywide service network and how badly it is damaged after the event.
