# Unidentified Eastgate Rift-Side Realm

## Summary
The Eastgate rift briefly exposes a different environment on the far side of the standing gateway. Current canon does not name or classify this realm, so the Atlas uses a descriptive working label rather than assigning it an invented identity.

## Description
Within the Eastgate gateway, the normal station view is replaced by a churning darkness filled with storm clouds, drifting ash, and distant points of orange fire. Shapes gather there before crossing into New Babylonia.

The environment is visible only through the rift during Chapter 1. No character physically enters it in the current manuscript.

## Environment / Conditions
- Churning darkness
- Storm clouds
- Drifting ash
- Distant orange points of fire
- Hostile entities visible gathering near the threshold
- Exact ground, sky, structures, scale, atmosphere, and gravity are not established

## Population / Control
- Rakers cross from this environment during the Eastgate event
- The unidentified frost-wreathed entity also crosses through the same gateway
- It is not established whether either species/entity originates there permanently or merely uses the location as a transit space

## Risks
- Known source/transit point for hostile incursions
- Environmental conditions beyond visual observation are unknown
- No safe human access or return method is established

## Narrative Use
- First current-manuscript visual glimpse of the space beyond a rift
- Provides a key visual design reference for the Eastgate gateway without prematurely defining the cosmology
- Should remain visually ominous and incomplete until later canon establishes more

## Canon Notes
- **Confirmed:** Storm clouds, drifting ash, churning darkness, distant orange fire, gathering hostile shapes.
- **Not confirmed:** That this is the Rakers' home realm, the frost entity's home realm, a single stable dimension, or the same destination behind every rift.
- **Intentionally unknown:** Name, geography, ecology, inhabitants, physics, relationship to other realms, and whether the view represents a permanent place or transient rift space.
- **Open questions:** Whether future rifts reveal the same environment and whether human travel into it is possible.
