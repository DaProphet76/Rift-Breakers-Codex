# Eastgate Bell Tower

## Summary
The Eastgate Bell Tower is a station structure beside the Train Seven platform. It rings the noon signal during the Chapter 1 departure sequence and collapses after a Raker and guard crash into its base, blocking Train Seven's forward track.

## Description
Chapter 1 does not provide a full architectural description. The structure supports a large bronze bell and is built from enough stone and timber that its collapse sends bronze, masonry, and broken wood across the platform and train.

## Environment / Conditions
- Positioned close to the Train Seven platform and forward rail line
- Audible across the station during noon-bell signals
- Structurally connected to a support that breaks during the Raker attack

## Population / Control
- No bell operator or maintenance staff is identified
- Its signals are part of Eastgate's public time/operational bell system

## Risks
- Heavy elevated structure over a crowded rail platform
- Structural failure during the attack causes major debris impact
- Collapse blocks Train Seven's forward route and contributes directly to the failed departure

## Narrative Use
- Marks the countdown to Train Seven's scheduled departure
- Becomes a physical symbol of normal station order collapsing under the rift attack
- Provides a major destruction beat for visual adaptation

## Canon Notes
- **Confirmed:** Large bronze bell; stone/timber structure; near platform/track; collapses after support damage; debris strikes Train Seven and blocks forward track.
- **Intentionally unknown:** Height, architectural style, mechanism, age, foundation design, and whether it is rebuilt.
- **Open questions:** Relationship between the tower's bells and the wider city bell network.
