# Vale Family Home

## Summary
The Vale family home is the Chapter 1 departure point for Darius Vale, Lysandra Vale, and young Sera. Only limited physical detail is currently established, so the entry records confirmed visual anchors without inventing a complete floor plan or façade.

## Description
The home opens onto a narrow Outer District street. Darius carries a heavily repaired travel chest from the house while Lysandra and Sera prepare inside. The immediate neighborhood contains patched houses, broken-stone streets, workshops, ration traffic, vendors, and views toward the fog-hidden Eastern Wall.

## Environment / Conditions
- Outer District residential street
- Dense neighboring construction
- Morning fog and industrial city noise
- Domestic possessions are practical and repeatedly repaired
- Family is preparing for permanent departure rather than a short trip

## Population / Control
- Occupied by Darius Vale, Lysandra Vale, and Sera Vale at the start of Chapter 1
- Kade Vale is unborn; Lysandra is approximately six months pregnant
- No landlord, ownership status, exact address, or building type is established

## Risks
- Outer District insecurity and institutional scrutiny
- A travel chest marked for eastern rail passage can attract attention or envy
- Home lies within a district that the Eastern Wall does not fully protect

## Narrative Use
- Establishes the Vale family's ordinary domestic life immediately before they abandon it
- Provides visual contrast between home-scale concerns and the catastrophic Eastgate sequence
- Should read as working-class Outer District housing rather than military, scavenger, or elite architecture

## Canon Notes
- **Confirmed:** Narrow street; family home in eastern Outer District; departure with repaired travel chest; domestic blankets/cooking pot packed for resettlement.
- **Intentionally unknown:** Exterior materials, number of rooms, floor plan, exact furnishings, ownership, and address.
- **Open questions:** Whether the home survives later events and whether the family ever returns.
